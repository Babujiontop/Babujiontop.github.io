Privacy Policy

MIXCOFARM MCFT Testnet

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. About this prototype

MIXCOFARM MCFT Testnet is an experimental application exploring Pi Network sign-in and digital payment and merchant-service concepts. It is a test prototype, not a licensed financial service.

2. Information processed

When you choose to sign in with Pi, the application requests your Pi username through the Pi SDK. Pi Network may process information according to its own policies.

The current prototype displays your username in your browser. It does not currently provide a completed backend for customer accounts, transaction records, or payment processing.

3. How information is used

Information available to this prototype is used to demonstrate sign-in and test the user interface. Do not submit passwords, secret wallet seeds, private keys, financial account details, or other sensitive information.

4. Storage and sharing

The current prototype has no application backend configured to store user profiles or transaction data. Pi Network, your browser, and hosting providers may process technical information under their own policies. We do not claim that these third-party systems collect no information.

5. Payments and testnet

This prototype does not currently process real-money payments. Testnet activity is experimental and must not be treated as a real financial transaction or a promise of token value.

6. Security

Do not share secret wallet seeds or private keys. The prototype is under development and is not intended for sensitive personal or financial information.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy must be updated as the application adds backend services, accounts, analytics, or payments. Continued use should be based on the policy then in effect.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the MIXCOFARM project developer through the contact details published with the app listing.

A dedicated contact email will be added when available.
