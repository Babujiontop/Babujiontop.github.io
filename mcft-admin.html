Participation Options

  • Voluntary partnership: independent retailers collaborate under agreed terms.
  • Stock purchase or contribution: documented inventory purchases or contributions.
  • Cooperative arrangement: retailers participate through a transparent cooperative structure.
  • Business acquisition: voluntary acquisition based on an agreed valuation and legal documents.

Fair Treatment of Existing Owners and Workers

Existing owners and workers may choose, under written agreements, to become:

  • Compensated suppliers.
  • Employees or store operators.
  • Professional market managers.
  • Investors or cooperative participants, where legally structured.

Participation must be voluntary. No coercive acquisition, forced contribution or undocumented transfer of ownership.

Security Requirements Before Production

  • Enable and test administrator authentication.
  • Require strong passwords and multi-factor authentication.
  • Use role-based access controls and least privilege.
  • Apply server-side authorization to every protected API.
  • Use CSRF protection and secure session management.
  • Apply rate limits and account lockout controls.
  • Protect personal data and financial information.
  • Conduct independent penetration testing and security review.
  • Complete applicable regulatory and legal reviews.
